Grow big, then prune back: cost-complexity pruning in action

Simulated data shaped like Boston medv vs lstat. CART grows a big tree on the training sample (black dots), then weakest-link pruning gives the nested subtrees that minimize RSS + α·|T|. Test points (grey) are never used for fitting.
Try: (1) slide α to 0: the full big tree chases the noise, so train MSE falls while test MSE typically rises. (2) Click New training sample a few times (α stays fixed): the split points jump around, since trees are unstable. (3) Each larger α gives a subtree of the previous one (nesting). The oracle button uses the test set, which you would not have in practice: there, choose α by cross-validation.
